### Full description — limit 4000

```
Your streak is no longer yours alone.

Pact matches you with one stranger. You both commit to a daily goal. You both
check in every day with a live photo. If either of you misses the deadline, the
shared streak drops to zero — for both of you.

That is the entire product. There is nothing else in it.

WHY A STRANGER

Habit apps fail because the only person you can disappoint is yourself, and you
have a lifetime of practice forgiving that person. A friend will let you off
too. Someone who owes you nothing, and is owed nothing back, will not.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Pick a goal — gym, study, reading, meditation, running, anything daily.
2. Get matched with one stranger who committed to showing up too.
3. Check in each day with a live photo. The camera opens straight to a frame.
4. Both of you check in, the streak grows. Either of you misses, it hits zero.
5. Keep the same partner until the streak breaks.

PROOF, NOT PROMISES

The camera opens to a live frame. There is no gallery button, no upload, no way
to submit yesterday's picture. Your partner sees the photo and the time you
sent it. That is all they ever see of you.

THE PACT LEVELS UP

Days 1-7 is a Level 1 Pact. Day 8 makes it Level 2, day 15 Level 3, and at day
31 it becomes Legendary — thirty-one consecutive days in which neither of two
strangers blinked.

YOUR RELIABILITY FOLLOWS YOU

Every check-in and every miss is counted for life. When you are matched again,
your next partner sees one number: the percentage of days you actually showed
up. It survives every broken streak, which is why it is worth protecting.

WHAT PACT DOES NOT HAVE

No chat. No followers. No likes, comments or reactions. No feed to scroll. No
friend requests. No leaderboards. No streak freezes. No ads.

What is left is one person who will find out, tomorrow morning, whether you
showed up.

WHO IT IS FOR

Students revising for exams. People in month two at the gym, when novelty has
worn off. Anyone with a daily practice — reading, meditation, running, an
instrument. Professionals with a habit they keep postponing until the quarter
calms down.

REQUIREMENTS

Android with a camera. An email address to verify your account. A goal small
enough that a bad day cannot excuse it.

Free. No subscription.
```
